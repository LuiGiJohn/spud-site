51 looks, shown on real footage.

Every built-in look in SPUD, in the five groups the app shows them in. Drag the divider on each picture to compare the shot before the look with the look applied. Every frame is real footage from a Fujifilm X-T5 or X-M5 or an iPhone.

  • Exposure firstSet each clip's exposure and white balance, then apply a look. Looks never change exposure or white balance.
  • Dial the strengthA look lands in its own nodes. Lower its Mix to use it at half strength, or trim any node.
  • Neutral versionsEach family has a Neutral look: its tone without its tint, for matching a set or as a base to grade from.
  • Log footageF-Log2, Apple Log and other log clips get a camera match under every look, so looks read the same as on Rec.709.
